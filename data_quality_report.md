## Notes

- **The 87 "arrival after departure" rows are not necessarily errors.**
  They're flagged by a simple string/time comparison that doesn't know
  about day boundaries. A stop where a train arrives at 23:50 and departs
  at 00:05 the next day is a legitimate short overnight halt, but shows up
  as "arrival > departure" under a same-day comparison. Recommend manual
  spot-check using the `day` field context rather than blanket deletion —
  the raw `day_offset` per stop is preserved in the source JSON if you
  want to re-derive this more precisely.
- Overall the dataset is clean: no fabricated placeholder values, no
  missing keys, no orphaned foreign keys, and totals per train are
  internally consistent with their route rows.
